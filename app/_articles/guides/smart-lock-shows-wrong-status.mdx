## Quick Answer

If the app shows the wrong smart lock status, physically check the door first, then force a status refresh from the app. If the door is actually locked but the app says unlocked, the issue is usually sync lag, app cache, hub connectivity, or calibration. If the app says locked but the deadbolt is not fully extended, treat it as a mechanical or calibration problem, not just a display bug.

Remote status is useful only when it matches reality. Until the issue is fixed, verify critical doors manually or with a trusted local person before relying on the app.

## Confirm the Real State

Start at the door if possible. Try the handle, look at the deadbolt, and use the thumb turn. Write down what the app says versus what the lock physically does.

If the lock operates correctly but the app is stale, focus on sync. If the lock does not fully lock or unlock, fix the mechanical issue first. A wrong status reading can be a symptom of incomplete bolt travel.

## Force a Fresh Status Check

Pull down to refresh in the app if supported. Close and reopen the app. Send a harmless command such as lock or unlock only if you are sure the door can move safely.

For Bluetooth locks, stand near the door. For Wi-Fi locks, check whether the lock is online. For hub-based locks, confirm the hub is online and that other devices are responding. If the status updates after a manual refresh, the problem is probably delay or packet loss, not calibration.

## Recalibrate the Lock

Smart locks can lose track of the deadbolt position after battery changes, jams, manual override, or repeated partial movements. Recalibration teaches the lock what fully locked and fully unlocked mean.

Look in the app for `Calibrate`, `Door handing`, `Reset position`, or `Lock orientation`. Follow the prompts with the door open unless the app tells you otherwise. After calibration, test app status after a keypad unlock, app unlock, thumb-turn lock, and auto-lock.

If calibration fails, check for friction. The lock cannot learn positions correctly when the bolt is dragging on the strike plate.

## Check Door Sensor Behavior

Some locks report both lock state and door state. If the app says the door is open when it is closed, auto-lock and status notifications may also be wrong.

Open and close the door while watching the app. If the door sensor lags or reports the wrong state, realign the magnet, recalibrate the sensor, replace the sensor battery if separate, and remove nearby metal or magnetic interference when possible.

If the lock status is correct but the door status is wrong, keep these issues separate. A door sensor problem does not always mean the deadbolt sensor is broken.

## Clear App or Hub Staleness

If only one phone shows the wrong status, the app may be caching stale data. Log out and back in, force close the app, or reinstall it if needed. Compare with another authorized user's phone before changing lock hardware.

If every phone shows the wrong status through a hub, refresh or reboot the hub. For Z-Wave and Zigbee systems, a repair or heal operation may improve routing if status updates are being lost.

## Account for Manual Operation

Some locks detect manual thumb-turn and key operation well; others update status only after electronic operation. If the status is wrong only after someone uses the physical key or thumb turn, the model may not have reliable manual-position sensing.

The workaround is to press the keypad, refresh the app, or use app/keypad operation when remote status matters. If accurate manual detection is critical, choose a lock known for better position sensing.

## Fix the Underlying Mechanical Cause

Status errors often follow incomplete bolt movement. If the app says locked but the bolt stops short, inspect alignment, strike depth, mounting screw tension, and batteries. If the app says unlocked but the bolt remains partly extended, recalibrate after the mechanism moves smoothly.

Do not hide a mechanical problem by refreshing the app until it says what you want. A deadbolt that does not travel fully is a security issue.

## When to Contact Support

Escalate if calibration does not hold, app and physical state diverge repeatedly, every user sees the wrong status, or status errors began after a firmware update. Provide the model, firmware version, hub type, screenshots, and a short video showing the physical lock state and app state together.
