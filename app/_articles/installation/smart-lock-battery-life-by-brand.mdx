## Quick Answer

Brand matters less than protocol, motor load, battery chemistry, and signal quality. A Schlage, Yale, August, Aqara, or Eufy lock can have excellent battery life when the door is aligned and the radio sleeps efficiently, or terrible battery life when Wi-Fi is weak and the bolt fights the strike plate.

Use the [Battery Life Calculator](/calculators/battery-life), [Signal Strength Calculator](/calculators/signal-strength), and [Door Compatibility Guide](/articles/guides/door-compatibility-guide) before blaming the brand.

## Battery Life Comparison Framework

| Factor | Battery impact | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Wi-Fi radio | High drain | Router distance, 2.4 GHz quality, bridge use |
| Z-Wave/Zigbee/Thread | Lower drain | Hub distance, repeater placement, mesh health |
| Deadbolt friction | High drain | Strike alignment, warped door, weatherstripping |
| Battery chemistry | Medium to high | Lithium vs alkaline, cold weather rating |
| Operation count | Medium | Rentals, cleaners, staff, family use |
| Weather | Medium | Cold reduces alkaline performance |

## Brand Notes

### Schlage

Schlage battery complaints often involve Wi-Fi models, cold weather, or heavy motor load. Check door alignment and signal before replacing the lock.

### Yale

Yale battery life varies by module. A Z-Wave or Zigbee module can behave very differently from Wi-Fi bridge workflows.

### August

Retrofit locks depend heavily on the existing deadbolt condition. If the thumbturn is stiff by hand, battery life will suffer.

### Aqara

Aqara models can perform well in Apple/HomeKit or Zigbee-style ecosystems, but exact battery life depends on model, region, and connected features.

### Eufy

Eufy locks often combine multiple unlock methods. Fingerprint, Wi-Fi bridge, camera, or video features can change the battery profile substantially.

## Troubleshooting Fast Battery Drain

1. Replace unknown batteries with name-brand alkaline or lithium.
2. Check whether the bolt extends smoothly with the door closed.
3. Measure signal quality with [Signal Strength Calculator](/calculators/signal-strength).
4. Reduce unnecessary Wi-Fi wakeups and app polling.
5. Update firmware only after reading model-specific notes.
6. Compare expected life in the [Battery Life Calculator](/calculators/battery-life).
