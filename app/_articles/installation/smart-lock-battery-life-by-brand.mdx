## Brand Is Only Part of Battery Life

Schlage, Yale, August, Aqara, and Eufy locks can all perform well when the door moves freely, the radio sleeps efficiently, and the right batteries are used. The same brands can also drain batteries quickly when Wi-Fi is weak, the bolt rubs the strike plate, weather is cold, or high-traffic doors wake the lock constantly.

Before blaming the badge on the front, check protocol, motor load, battery chemistry, signal quality, and operation count. The [Battery Life Calculator](/calculators/battery-life) and [Signal Strength Calculator](/calculators/signal-strength) are better starting points than brand reputation alone.

## What Usually Changes by Brand

Schlage battery complaints often involve Wi-Fi models, cold weather, or a motor working against door friction. Yale varies heavily by module: a Z-Wave or Zigbee module behaves differently from a Wi-Fi bridge workflow. August retrofit locks depend on the existing deadbolt; if the thumb turn is stiff by hand, battery life will suffer.

Aqara models can do well in Apple/HomeKit or Zigbee-style ecosystems, but exact results depend on region, model, and enabled features. Eufy locks often combine several unlock methods, and fingerprint, Wi-Fi bridge, camera, or video features can all change the battery profile.

## The Checks That Matter More Than the Logo

A Wi-Fi radio usually drains more than Z-Wave, Zigbee, Thread, or Bluetooth because the lock has to maintain a more demanding network connection. Weak signal makes this worse. A bridge or hub placed poorly can create repeated retries even when the lock looks online.

Mechanical load is just as important. If the deadbolt scrapes, stops short, or needs a push on the door, the motor uses more current on every cycle. Cold weather reduces alkaline battery performance. Heavy guest, cleaner, staff, or family traffic shortens replacement intervals regardless of brand.

## Replacement Policy by Property Type

For an owner-occupied home, replacing batteries at the low-battery warning or around 20 to 30 percent is usually fine. For Airbnb or high-turnover rentals, replace before busy periods and use a higher threshold, such as around 40 percent. Long-term rentals can tie battery checks to inspections or turnover. Multifamily and commercial deployments should standardize dashboard alerts or work orders.

If batteries drain quickly after replacement, test the bolt with the door open and closed, check signal at the door, review app polling or Wi-Fi wakeups, and confirm firmware notes for the exact model before replacing the lock.
