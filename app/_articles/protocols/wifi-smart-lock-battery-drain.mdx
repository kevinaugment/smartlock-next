## Quick Answer

Wi-Fi smart locks drain batteries faster because the radio uses more power than sleeping mesh protocols. The problem gets worse when signal is weak, the bolt rubs the strike plate, firmware wakes the radio often, or a rental door sees heavy guest and cleaner use.

Estimate the real replacement schedule with the [Battery Life Calculator](/calculators/battery-life), then check link quality with the [Signal Strength Calculator](/calculators/signal-strength).

## Battery Drain Cause Table

| Cause | What happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Weak Wi-Fi | Lock retries cloud or app communication | Move router, add access point, improve 2.4 GHz signal |
| Motor drag | Bolt needs extra torque | Align strike plate and test bolt with door closed |
| Cold weather | Alkaline batteries lose capacity | Use better batteries and replace earlier |
| Heavy use | More motor cycles and radio wakeups | Plan scheduled battery replacement |
| Firmware or app polling | Lock wakes too often | Update firmware, reduce unnecessary integrations |
| Direct Wi-Fi design | Radio is power-hungry | Consider Zigbee, Z-Wave, Thread, or bridge-based architecture |

## Wi-Fi vs Mesh Power Behavior

Battery-powered Zigbee, Z-Wave, and Thread locks can sleep aggressively and wake for short messages. Wi-Fi locks often need more power to associate, authenticate, send status, and reconnect after network changes.

This does not mean every Wi-Fi lock is bad. It means Wi-Fi is less forgiving when signal is poor or the door is used like a commercial access point.

## Fixes Before Replacing the Lock

1. Replace unknown batteries with fresh name-brand cells.
2. Confirm the deadbolt moves smoothly by hand.
3. Measure signal quality near the door, not beside the router.
4. Disable unnecessary automations that poll status constantly.
5. Update firmware after checking release notes.
6. Track operations per day for a week.
7. Compare the result against mesh or bridge-based alternatives.

## Rental Maintenance Rules

For Airbnb and vacation rentals, replace batteries before high-occupancy periods instead of waiting for low-battery alerts. For long-term rentals, align replacement with inspection or turnover. For portfolios, choose a protocol and battery policy that staff can repeat without diagnosing every door from scratch.

If the lock is remote and guest access is mission-critical, pair battery planning with the [Emergency Backup Planner](/calculators/emergency-backup).

## When to Switch Protocols

Switch away from direct Wi-Fi when batteries routinely fail before the expected maintenance window, signal cannot be improved, the property has many doors, or lockouts create expensive emergency labor.

Zigbee, Z-Wave, Thread, or a bridge-based design can reduce radio drain, but they introduce hub and coverage planning. Use the [Protocol Selection Wizard](/calculators/protocol-wizard) before changing hardware.
