## Quick Answer

Choose Z-Wave when range, mature hubs, battery life, and repeatable multi-door operation matter most. Choose Matter when cross-platform Apple, Google, Alexa, SmartThings, or Home Assistant compatibility is the bigger buying risk.

Use the [Protocol Selection Wizard](/calculators/protocol-wizard) first, then confirm building range with the [Signal Strength Calculator](/calculators/signal-strength).

## Z-Wave vs Matter Comparison

| Decision area | Z-Wave smart locks | Matter smart locks |
|---|---|---|
| Core role | Device protocol for locks, sensors, and hubs | Interoperability layer across ecosystems |
| Typical radio | Sub-GHz Z-Wave | Thread or Wi-Fi, depending on model |
| Best fit | Apartments, larger homes, property portfolios | Mixed smart-home ecosystems |
| Hub need | Z-Wave hub or controller | Matter controller; Thread models also need a border router |
| Battery profile | Usually strong because locks sleep aggressively | Good on Thread, weaker on Wi-Fi |
| Maturity | Long smart-lock track record | Improving quickly, but model support varies |

## When Z-Wave Is the Better Lock Choice

Z-Wave is usually stronger when the deployment problem is physical reliability: distance from hub to lock, thick walls, corridors, concrete, or many doors that need the same maintenance playbook.

It is a practical default for landlords, installers, and property managers who already use SmartThings, Hubitat, Alarm.com, Home Assistant with Z-Wave, or another controller that supports secure lock inclusion.

## When Matter Is the Better Lock Choice

Matter is stronger when the risk is ecosystem lock-in. If a household or property may mix Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings, or Home Assistant, Matter can reduce platform-specific dead ends.

The important caveat: "Matter smart lock" does not always mean Thread. Some locks use Matter over Thread, while others use Matter over Wi-Fi. That difference affects battery life, hub requirements, and network planning.

## Rental and Enterprise Rules

| Use case | Better starting point | Why |
|---|---|---|
| One owner-occupied home | Matter | Easier mixed-platform resale and family access |
| Thick-wall apartment | Z-Wave | Sub-GHz range can be more forgiving |
| Multi-property rental | Z-Wave | Mature hub workflows and repeatability |
| Premium Apple-first unit | Matter or HomeKit | Better resident experience if fallback access exists |
| Enterprise access control | Neither by default | Audit logs, staff lifecycle, and integrations usually matter more |

## Buyer Mistakes to Avoid

- Buying a Matter lock without checking whether it uses Thread or Wi-Fi.
- Assuming Z-Wave works without a compatible hub.
- Comparing protocols before checking door fit.
- Ignoring guest-code capacity, audit logs, and backup access for rentals.
- Treating consumer ecosystem compatibility as a substitute for commercial access control.
