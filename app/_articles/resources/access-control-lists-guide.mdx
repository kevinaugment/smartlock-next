## The Rule of Who, Where, When

**Access Control Lists (ACLs)** define permissions.
*   **Who**: User Group (e.g., "Cleaners").
*   **Where**: Zone (e.g., "All Units" vs "Laundry Room").
*   **When**: Schedule (e.g., "M-F, 9am-5pm").

### Best Practices
*   **Role-Based Access**: Never assign permissions to a *person*. Assign them to a *role*, then put the person in the role.
*   **Least Privilege**: Give users access *only* to what they need.
