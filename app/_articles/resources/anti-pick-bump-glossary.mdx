## Executive Summary

While movies show spies picking locks with paperclips in 3 seconds, reality is different. However, techniques like **Lock Bumping** allow unskilled attackers to open standard locks in seconds with zero skill.

The "Smart" advantage isn't just Wi-Fi; it's the ability to **remove the keyway entirely**, rendering 90% of physical attacks impossible.

---

## The Threat Matrix

### 1. Lock Bumping (The "Key Gun")
*   **The Attack**: A "Bump Key" (cut to the deepest depth) is inserted and tapped with a mallet. The vibration transfers energy to the driver pins (Newton's Cradle effect), momentarily jumping them above the shear line.
*   **Skill Level**: **Zero** (YouTube tutorial).
*   **Vulnerability**: Any standard pin-tumbler lock (Schlage C-Keyway, Kwikset KW1).
*   **Smart Defense**: **Keyless Locks** (e.g., Yale Assure Key Free) are 100% immune. No hole = No bump.

### 2. Lock Picking (Raking & SPP)
*   **The Attack**: Manipulating pins one by one (SPP) or scrubbing them (Raking) to find the shear line.
*   **Skill Level**: Moderate to High.
*   **Smart Defense**: **Spool Pins** and **Serrated Pins**. These security pins are shaped like spools; they "catch" on the cylinder wall if lifted incorrectly, giving false feedback and jamming the rotation.

### 3. Drilling
*   **The Attack**: Using a cobalt drill bit to destroy the shear line or mounting screws.
*   **Skill Level**: Low (Brute force).
*   **Smart Defense**: **Anti-Drill Plates**. Hardened steel or ceramic plates (Ball Bearings) inserted in front of the cylinder spin *with* the drill bit, preventing it from biting into the metal.

## Advanced Countermeasures

### 1. Kwikset SmartKey™ (The Wafer Solution)
SmartKey cylinders don't use pins. They use **side-locking bars** and wafers.
*   **Pros**: Mechanically immune to traditional bumping and picking.
*   **Cons**: Susceptible to "Force" attacks (screwdriver torque) in earlier generations (Gen 1/2). Gen 3 is significantly tougher.

### 2. Disc Detainer (The Nuclear Option)
Found in high-end locks like **Abloy** (and some custom smart lock cores).
*   **Mechanism**: Rotating discs instead of lifting pins.
*   **Security**: Requires specialized, expensive tools to pick. Virtually bump-proof.

### 3. Sidebars (Schlage Primus)
A secondary locking bar that checks for "side cuts" on the key.
*   **Security**: Raises the difficulty level to "Expert."

## Real World Risk Assessment

Should you pay extra for "Anti-Pick"?
*   **Urban Apartment**: **Yes**. Bumping is preferred because it is quiet and leaves no evidence (clean entry).
*   **Rural/Suburban Home**: **No**. Statistics show burglars kick doors. Focus on **ANSI Grade 1** (Impact Resistance) rather than pick resistance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if a Keyless Lock dies?
Without a keyhole, you use the **9V Jump Start**. Hold a 9V battery to the two external terminals. This powers the chip enough to enter your code. It does NOT charge the lock.

### Is biometric safer than a key?
Yes. keys can be copied at a kiosk in Home Depot. Fingerprints cannot (easily). Removing the keyway removes the easiest vector for unauthorized duplication.

### Can I upgrade my cylinder?
Yes. Most smart locks (August, Level, Yale) allow you to swap the cylinder. You can install a high-security **Mul-T-Lock** or **Medeco** cylinder into a standard smart lock housing for maximum protection.
