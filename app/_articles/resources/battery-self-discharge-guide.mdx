## The Hidden Drain

### Self-Discharge
Every battery loses charge just sitting on a shelf.
*   **Alkaline**: ~2-3% per year.
*   **NiMH**: ~1-2% *per day* (older types) or 10-20% per year (LSD). Not recommended for locks.
*   **Lithium (Primary)**: <1% per year. Best for vacation homes.

### Cold Weather Impact
Cold slows down the chemical reaction inside a battery, increasing its internal resistance.
*   **Symptom**: Lock moves slowly or fails to fully extend the bolt on cold mornings.
*   **False Low Battery**: The voltage drop under load (when motor runs) triggers the "Low Battery" warning, even if capacity remains.
