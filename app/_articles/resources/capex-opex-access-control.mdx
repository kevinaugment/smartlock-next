## Expenditure Types

### CapEx (Capital Expenditure)
The upfront cost to buy and install.
*   **Hardware**: Lock, Hub, Gateway.
*   **Installation**: Wiring, Door Prep, Setup.
*   *Budget Strategy*: One-time project budget.

### OpEx (Operational Expenditure)
The ongoing cost to keep it running.
*   **Batteries**: Recurring.
*   **SaaS Fees**: Monthly software limits.
*   **Support**: IT time to reset passwords/troubleshoot.
*   *Budget Strategy*: Annual maintenance budget.

## 2026 Planning Notes

Reference tables stay useful only when you add the hidden operational items around them: batteries, truck rolls, credential support, subscriptions, and rework after a bad retrofit.

For guest or tenant access, always confirm offline keypad behavior, code rotation speed, and what happens if the internet or PMS integration is unavailable.

## How to Use This Reference

1. Confirm the exact door type, lock family, controller path, and operating environment you are designing for.
2. Eliminate options that fail the mechanical, compliance, offline-access, or lifecycle-cost requirements that matter most for this deployment.
3. Validate the final choice against the current manufacturer documentation and a test on the real door whenever the consequence of failure is high.
