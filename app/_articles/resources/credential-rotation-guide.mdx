## Why Rotate?

Static codes are a security risk. They get shared, written on post-it notes, or captured by hidden cameras.

### Rotation Schedule
*   **High Security (Server Room)**: Every 30-90 days.
*   **Medium Security (Office)**: Every 6 months or upon employee exit.
*   **Low Security (Home)**: Once a year.

### The "Kill Switch"
Always have a master code or admin app that can instantly revoke a credential if it's compromised.
