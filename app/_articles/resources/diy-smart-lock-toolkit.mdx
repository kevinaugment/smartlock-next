## The Essentials List

### Must-Haves
*   **Phillips Head Screwdriver (#2)**: For removing old lock and installing new one. Do *not* use a power drill for final tightening (strips screws).
*   **Tape Measure**: To verify backset and door thickness.
*   **Painter's Tape**: To hold the latch in place during install.

### For Fresh Installs (Drilling)
*   **Hole Saw Kit (2-1/8" & 1")**: Specifically for doors.
*   **Chisel (1")**: To mortise the latch faceplate flush.
*   **Hammer**: For the chisel.
*   **Utility Knife**: To score paint around the old latch.
