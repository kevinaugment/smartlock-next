## Backup Access Matrix

| Method | Used By | Pros | Cons |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **9V Battery Contacts** | Yale, Samsung, Schlage (Keypad) | Universal, cheap battery | Looks industrial, hard to hold while typing code |
| **USB-C / Micro-USB Port** | Eufy, Lockly, Aqara | Modern, power bank accessible | Need a cable + power bank |
| **Physical Key Override** | August, Level, Schlage Encode | 100% reliable | Can be picked/bumped, key management |
| **Mechanical Code (Button)** | Simplex, Kaba | No power needed | Expensive, commercial look |

### Key Takeaways
*   **Always Carry a Key**: If your lock has a keyway, keep a spare key hidden outside or in your car.
*   **Power Bank**: If your lock has USB-C backup, a small lipstick-sized power bank in your glovebox is a lifesaver.
