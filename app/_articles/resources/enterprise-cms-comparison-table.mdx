## Cloud Management Systems (CMS)

| Feature | Yale Accentra | RemoteLock | Salto KS | Brivo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **User Capacity** | 500+ | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Site Hierarchy** | Strong | Basic | Strong | Strong |
| **API Openness** | Low | High | Medium | Medium |
| **Mobile Credential** | Yes (Bluetooth) | No | Yes | Yes |
| **Price Tier** | Premium | Mid-Range | Premium | Premium |

### Key Takeaways
*   **RemoteLock**: Best for decentralized portfolios (Airbnbs + small offices).
*   **Salto KS**: Best for pure office/commercial with heavy traffic.
*   **Yale Accentra**: Best for multi-family housing.
