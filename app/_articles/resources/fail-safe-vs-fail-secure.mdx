## The Difference Explained

### Fail-Safe (Power to Lock)
*   **Unlocked when power creates**: The lock requires electricity to stay locked.
*   **Power Outage**: Unlocks automatically.
*   **Use Case**: Fire exits, office main entries (life safety).
*   **Type**: Magnetic locks (Maglocks).

### Fail-Secure (Power to Open)
*   **Locked when power creates**: The lock requires electricity to unlock.
*   **Power Outage**: Stays locked.
*   **Use Case**: Server rooms, home front doors (security).
*   **Type**: Electric Strikes, Smart Deadbolts.

## 2026 Planning Notes

Code, insurance, and AHJ interpretation can differ from the way vendors summarize standards on product pages. Use the exact listing and local requirement as the final authority.

Always verify the current listing, code interpretation, or insurer requirement against the exact device, door assembly, and jurisdiction where you plan to use it.

## How to Use This Reference

1. Confirm the exact door type, lock family, controller path, and operating environment you are designing for.
2. Eliminate options that fail the mechanical, compliance, offline-access, or lifecycle-cost requirements that matter most for this deployment.
3. Validate the final choice against the current manufacturer documentation and a test on the real door whenever the consequence of failure is high.
