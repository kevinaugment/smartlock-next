## The Difference Explained

### Fail-Safe (Power to Lock)
*   **Unlocked when power creates**: The lock requires electricity to stay locked.
*   **Power Outage**: Unlocks automatically.
*   **Use Case**: Fire exits, office main entries (life safety).
*   **Type**: Magnetic locks (Maglocks).

### Fail-Secure (Power to Open)
*   **Locked when power creates**: The lock requires electricity to unlock.
*   **Power Outage**: Stays locked.
*   **Use Case**: Server rooms, home front doors (security).
*   **Type**: Electric Strikes, Smart Deadbolts.
