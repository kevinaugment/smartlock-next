## Executive Summary

In 2026, the cost to install a smart lock is no longer just "an hour of labor." It is a specialized service involving door alignment, fire-code compliance (NFPA 80), and network provisioning.

**The Golden Numbers**:
*   **Residential Average**: **$212 - $503** (Total Project Cost).
*   **Commercial Average**: **$300 - $800** (Per Door).

This guide breaks down *exactly* what you are paying for, so you can leverage this expertise without getting fleeced.

---

## 2026 Residential Pricing Matrix

For a standard single-family home or Airbnb property.

| Service Item | Cost Range | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Service Call (Trip Charge)** | **$65 - $150** | The cost to roll the truck. Higher in Urban zones (NYC/LA) due to parking/traffic. |
| **Retrofit Labor**<br>*(Swap existing lock)* | **$100 - $175** | Assumes the door is pre-drilled and aligned. 20-30 min job. |
| **Fresh Install Labor**<br>*(Drilling new holes)* | **$150 - $250** | Requires boring a 2-1/8" hole and chiseling the frame. High risk = High cost. |
| **Door Adjustment** | **$50 - $150** | **Critical**. If the door drags, the smart lock motor burns out. Pros fix the hinges first. |
| **Wi-Fi/App Setup** | **$50 Flat** | Many locksmiths now charge "Tech Fees" to pair the lock to your phone. |

### The "Amazon Installation" Warning
"Handyman" services (Amazon Home Services) often quote **$99**.
*   **The Risk**: They are often unlicensed contractors. If they drill the hole crooked, your warranty is void and your door is ruined.
*   **Recommendation**: Use a **Bonded Locksmith** (ALOA Certified) for any fresh drilling.

## 2026 Commercial Pricing Matrix

For Offices, Retail, and Multi-Family Common Areas.

| Service Item | Cost Range | Commercial Complexity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade 1 Hardware Install** | **$150 - $300** | Installing heavy-duty cylindrical or mortise locks (Schlage ND/L Series). |
| **Electric Strike Cut-In** | **$450 - $800** | Cutting the steel frame to install a wired strike. Requires low-voltage license. |
| **Panic Bar Integration** | **$600+** | Retrofitting a crash bar with an electrified latch retraction kit. |
| **System Commissioning** | **$125/hr** | Programming the access control server, testing keycards, setting schedules. |

### Why Commercial Costs 3x More
1.  **Fire Code (NFPA 80)**: You cannot modify a fire door in the field without recertification. One wrong drill hole voids the label.
2.  **Steel Frames**: Drilling concrete-filled steel frames requires specialized tooling.
3.  **Low Voltage**: Wired locks require running cable through walls/ceilings (Plenum rated).

## The "Bait-and-Switch" Red Flags

Protect your budget by asking these questions **before** dispatching:

1.  **"Is the Trip Charge included in the labor quote?"**
    *   *Scam*: Quoting "$29 Service Call" ($29 to arrive, $300 to install).
2.  **"Do you drill steel doors?"** (For commercial)
    *   *Reality Check*: Many residential locksmiths break their bits on commercial steel.
3.  **"Will you calibrate the strike plate?"**
    *   *Requirement*: A smart lock needs a "butter-smooth" throw. If they don't file the strike plate, don't pay.

## DIY vs Professional: The Break-Even Point

*   **1 Lock**: DIY. (2 hours learning curve + $50 tools > $200 pro install).
*   **10 Locks**: Pro. ($1500 bulk rate < 20 hours of your time).
*   **Mortise Locks**: **ALWAYS PRO**. These are complex puzzle boxes. Opening one up often creates a springing explosion of parts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is re-keying so expensive?
Re-keying ($25-$50/cylinder) requires disassembling the entire lock to change tiny distinct pins (0.003" tolerance). It is precision work. For high-volume, consider "SmartKey" (Kwikset) or Interchangeable Cores (IC) which allow re-keying in seconds without tools.

### Do locksmiths warranty smart locks?
Only if they sell it to you. If you buy a Yale lock on Amazon and hire a pro to install it, they warranty the **Installation** (it won't fall off), but if the motor dies in a week, you deal with Yale support, not the locksmith.

### Can a locksmith hack my smart lock?
No more than anyone else. However, ensure you perform a **Factory Reset** and claim "Master Admin" rights immediately after they leave. Never leave the installer's phone paired as an admin.
