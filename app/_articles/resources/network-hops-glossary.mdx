## The Cost of Hops

A "Hop" is when a message jumps from Device A -> Device B -> Hub.
*   **Zigbee Max Hops**: ~15-30 (depending on profile).
*   **Z-Wave Max Hops**: 4.

### Impact on Latency
Each hop adds delay (~10-50ms).
*   **1 Hop**: Instant response.
*   **4 Hops**: Noticeable lag (human perception threshold ~100ms).
*   **Too Many Hops**: Packet loss and "Lock Not Responding" errors.

## 2026 Planning Notes

Published range, battery, and interoperability numbers are planning inputs only. Walls, radio congestion, border routers, and firmware policy still change the field result materially.

Treat local radio health, border-router or hub placement, and fallback behavior during internet loss as first-class design inputs, not afterthoughts.
