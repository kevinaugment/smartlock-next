## Annual Maintenance Estimates (Per 100 Units)

| Cost Item | Frequency | Unit Cost | Annual Total |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Battery Replacement** | 1.5x / Year | $2.00 (4xAA) | $300 |
| **Labor (Battery Swap)** | 1.5x / Year | $15 (15 min) | $2,250 |
| **Firmware Updates** | 1x / Year | $0 (OTA) | $0 |
| **Failed Unit Swap** | 2% Rate | $250 (Hardware + Labor) | $500 |
| **Platform Subscription** | Monthly | $5 / Unit | $6,000 |

### Key Takeaways
*   **Labor is the killer**: Paying someone to drive to a site to change batteries costs 10x more than the batteries.
*   **Subscription**: Cloud fees often exceed hardware costs within 3-4 years.

## 2026 Planning Notes

Reference tables stay useful only when you add the hidden operational items around them: batteries, truck rolls, credential support, subscriptions, and rework after a bad retrofit.

2. Eliminate options that fail the mechanical, compliance, offline-access, or lifecycle-cost requirements that matter most for this deployment.
