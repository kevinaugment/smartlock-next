## Quick Answer

Mobile keys beat keycards when the hotel has enough room nights, front desk volume, and PMS integration maturity to offset hardware, software, and support costs. For smaller properties, keypad or card-based smart locks may deliver faster payback with less guest education.

Use [Hotel ROI Calculator](/calculators/hotel-roi), [Access Capacity Calculator](/calculators/access-capacity), and [PoE Power Budget Calculator](/calculators/poe-power) before choosing a platform.

## ROI Drivers

| Driver | Mobile key impact |
|---|---|
| Front desk labor | Reduces key encoding and replacement workload |
| Guest experience | Supports direct-to-room check-in |
| Keycard cost | Reduces plastic card purchasing and disposal |
| Lockout calls | Can fall if mobile keys are reliable, rise if app onboarding fails |
| PMS integration | Determines whether automation is real or manual |
| Backup operations | Still needs cards, PINs, or staff override |

## When Mobile Keys Win

- High check-in volume creates front desk bottlenecks.
- PMS integration is stable and supported.
- Guests already use a hotel app or loyalty flow.
- Staff have a clear fallback process for dead phones and failed credentials.
- The property can train housekeeping and maintenance on role-based access.

## When Keycards Still Win

- Budget hotels with low app adoption.
- Properties without PMS integration resources.
- Small inns where front desk workload is minimal.
- Buildings with existing card infrastructure that is not near replacement.

## Smart Lock Deployment Checklist

1. Calculate current keycard, encoder, labor, and lockout costs.
2. Confirm PMS and mobile key vendor integration.
3. Test check-in, room move, late checkout, and checkout expiration.
4. Define staff override access.
5. Keep accessible and fire-compliant door hardware.
6. Pilot one floor before replacing the fleet.
