## Quick Answer

Multifamily smart locks need fleet operations, not one-door convenience. The core requirements are resident credential lifecycle, staff/vendor access, auditability, privacy, battery maintenance, and a support process for lockouts.

## Recommended Architecture

For unit doors, prefer locks with local credential storage and clear admin workflows. For common doors, evaluate commercial access control, fire code, ADA requirements, and emergency egress before using residential smart lock hardware.

A practical deployment path:

1. Pilot 5-10 doors across different floor plans.
2. Measure battery, signal, support tickets, and resident adoption.
3. Define credential naming and deletion policy.
4. Create staff/vendor role templates.
5. Standardize on 1-2 lock models, not a mixed fleet.
6. Document emergency access and privacy notice language.

## Resident Credential Policy

- One named credential per resident.
- No shared household master PIN unless required by the platform.
- Delete or suspend credentials at move-out.
- Separate leasing, maintenance, cleaning, and emergency roles.
- Review access logs only for legitimate operational or security events.
