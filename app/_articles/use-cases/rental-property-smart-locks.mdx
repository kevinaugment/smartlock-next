## Quick Answer

Rental property smart locks should be planned around turnover, maintenance, and liability. The best landlord setup uses unique tenant credentials, separate vendor access, documented backup entry, predictable battery replacement, and a lock model that fits the door without custom work.

## Landlord Deployment Rules

1. Keep owner/admin codes separate from tenant codes.
2. Delete tenant app access and PINs at move-out.
3. Use vendor codes with time windows for cleaners and maintenance.
4. Keep original hardware for apartments where lease or HOA rules matter.
5. Replace batteries before they become tenant emergencies.
6. Document access-log retention and privacy expectations.

## Recommended Architecture

For one or two units, a keypad deadbolt with app management may be enough. For a portfolio, standardize on one platform, one battery policy, one naming convention, and one emergency access procedure. Mixed lock fleets create support cost that rarely appears in the shelf price.
