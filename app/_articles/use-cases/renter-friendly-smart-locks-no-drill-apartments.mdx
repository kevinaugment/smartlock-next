## Quick Answer

The safest renter-friendly smart lock is a retrofit lock that keeps the exterior keyway unchanged and installs on the inside thumbturn. It gives app or auto-unlock convenience while avoiding drilling, rekeying, or visible changes that may violate a lease.

Use the [Retrofit Advisor](/calculators/retrofit-advisor) and [Door Fit Checker](/calculators/door-fit) before buying. If you manage rental units rather than rent one apartment, start with [Smart Locks for Long-Term Rentals](/articles/use-cases/long-term-rental-strategy).

## Renter-Friendly Lock Types

| Type | Lease risk | Best for | Watch-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interior retrofit | Low | Apartments where exterior hardware must stay | Needs compatible thumbturn |
| Keypad deadbolt replacement | Medium | Landlord-approved upgrades | Changes exterior hardware |
| Smart lockbox | Low to medium | Backup key storage | Lower perceived security |
| Adhesive or clamp retrofit | Low | Temporary installs | Fit varies by deadbolt shape |

## Apartment Buying Checklist

- Read the lease section on locks, keys, and alterations.
- Keep original hardware and screws in a labeled bag.
- Avoid drilling, filing strike plates, or changing the keyway without permission.
- Make sure emergency access still works for the landlord where legally required.
- Test manual key operation after installation.
- Remove app users and reset the device before moving out.

## Best Fit Scenarios

### Renter Wants Convenience

Choose an interior retrofit with phone unlock and auto-lock. Add a keypad only if guests, cleaners, or roommates need entry without your phone.

### Landlord Approves Upgrade

A full keypad deadbolt can work if the landlord agrees and receives the required key or admin access. Use [Installation Cost Calculator](/calculators/installation-cost) for labor planning.

### Short-Term Sublet or Corporate Housing

Use time-limited PINs and clear checkout deletion. Pair with [Guest Code Planner](/calculators/guest-code) and [Delete Smart Lock User](/articles/guides/delete-smart-lock-user).

## Common Mistakes

- Buying a replacement deadbolt before checking lease rules.
- Forgetting that many apartment doors use mortise or multi-point hardware.
- Removing landlord emergency access.
- Depending on auto-unlock without a keypad or physical key backup.
