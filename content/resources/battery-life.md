## Battery Chemistry Comparison

| Type | Voltage (V) | Typical Capacity (mAh) | Cold Performance | Self-Discharge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alkaline AA** | 1.5V | 2500 - 2800 | Poor | Low |
| **Lithium AA (L91)** | 1.5V | 3000 - 3500 | Excellent | Very Low |
| **CR123A (Lithium)** | 3.0V | 1500 - 1600 | Good | Very Low |
| **9V Alkaline** | 9.0V | 550 - 600 | Poor | Low |
| **NiMH AA (Rechargeable)** | 1.2V | 2000 - 2500 | Fair | High |

### Why Lithium Wins
*   **Voltage Sag**: Alkaline voltage drops linearly. Lithium holds ~1.5V until 95% depleted.
*   **Cold**: At -20°C (-4°F), Alkaline loses ~50% capacity. Lithium loses ~10%.
*   **Leakage**: Lithium won't leak and ruin your expensive lock.

## The Hidden Drain

### Self-Discharge
Every battery loses charge just sitting on a shelf.
*   **Alkaline**: ~2-3% per year.
*   **NiMH**: ~1-2% *per day* (older types) or 10-20% per year (LSD). Not recommended for locks.
*   **Lithium (Primary)**: <1% per year. Best for vacation homes.

### Cold Weather Impact
Cold slows down the chemical reaction inside a battery, increasing its internal resistance.
*   **Symptom**: Lock moves slowly or fails to fully extend the bolt on cold mornings.
*   **False Low Battery**: The voltage drop under load (when motor runs) triggers the "Low Battery" warning, even if capacity remains.
