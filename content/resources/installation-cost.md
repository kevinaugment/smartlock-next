### Comparison: DIY vs Pro
*   **DIY**: $0 labor, 30-60 mins per lock. Risk: Voided warranty if door modified incorrectly.
*   **Handyman**: ~70% of Locksmith rate. Good for replacement, bad for fresh drilling.
*   **Pro Locksmith**: Highest cost, guarantees alignment (critical for battery life).

## Common "Gotchas"

### 1. Door Alignment Correction ($50-100/door)
Smart locks require effortless deadbolt throw. If your current lock requires you to "push/pull/lift" the door to lock it, a smart lock will fail (jam) or drain batteries in weeks. Adjusting hinges/strike plates is extra labor.

### 2. Door Thickness Adapter ($15-30 kit)
Standard residential doors are 1-3/8" to 1-3/4". Thick entry doors (2" +) require long-screw kits or spindle extensions often not included in the box.

### 3. Wi-Fi Bridge / Hub ($30-80 unit)
"Bluetooth only" locks are cheap but need a bridge for remote access. Don't forget this hardware cost.

### 4. Re-keying ($20-30/cylinder)
To use one physical key for all new locks, you must pay for re-keying or buy "Keyed Alike" sets upfront.
