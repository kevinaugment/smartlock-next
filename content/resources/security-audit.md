## A156.36 (Auxiliary Locks)

| Grade | Cycle Test (Uses) | Impact Test (Blows) | Strength |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grade 1 (Best)** | 250,000 cycles | 10 blows (40 ft-lbs) | Heavy Duty Commercial |
| **Grade 2 (Better)** | 150,000 cycles | 5 blows (40 ft-lbs) | Light Commercial / High-End Resi |
| **Grade 3 (Good)** | 100,000 cycles | 2 blows (40 ft-lbs) | Standard Residential |

### Key Takeaways
*   **Look for the Badge**: If a lock doesn't advertise its Grade, it's likely uncertified (Grade 3 or worse).
*   **Deadbolt vs Handle**: A Grade 1 deadbolt is useless if the door frame (strike) is weak.

## Attack Methods

### Lock Picking
Manipulating pins individually to mimic a key.
*   **Countermeasure**: "Spool pins" or "Mushroom pins" that get stuck if lifted incorrectly.

### Key Bumping
Inserting a specially cut key and hitting it to jump all pins at once.
*   **Countermeasure**: Sidebar mechanisms (Primus, Medeco) or "Bump-proof" pin stacks.

### Drill Resistance
Drilling out the shear line.
*   **Countermeasure**: Hardened steel ball bearings inserted into the cylinder face.
